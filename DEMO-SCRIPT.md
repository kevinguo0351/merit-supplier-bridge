# Merit Supplier Bridge — 90-second demo script

Prototype: open `index.html` by double-click (works offline, no server needed).
Language toggle (EN / ES / 中文) is top-right; "Reset demo data" restores the seeded state.
All numbers on screen are computed live from the illustrative inputs — nothing is hard-coded.

---

## The 90-second walkthrough

**0:00 — Frame the problem (Triage tab, default).**
Say: "Merit has ~1,030 suppliers in 42 countries. The usual instinct is to manage the
suppliers you spend the most on. We think that's the wrong axis."
Click the **Rank by: Spend** toggle. Point at the highlighted top-8 rows — they're the big
US / Ireland / Germany anchors, almost all in the blue **Harvest** quadrant.
Read the live readout aloud: "Top 8 by spend includes **0 of 5** Support-first suppliers."

**0:25 — Flip the axis.**
Click **Rank by: Risk to Merit**. The table re-sorts; the escalation (red flag) supplier jumps
to the top, then the small Tijuana and Dongguan plants. Say: "Risk = irreplaceable × invisible.
These are the sole-source MX and CN manufacturers Merit can neither see nor lose — and they're
low-spend, so spend-ranking hides them."
Point at the **2×2 matrix**: x = invisibility, y = irreplaceability, dot size = spend. The big
dots sit bottom-left and right (Harvest / Light touch); the Support-first corner (top-right) is
full of small dots.

**0:45 — Show the fix: make an invisible supplier visible (Supplier workspace tab).**
Supplier defaults to "Moldeados Precisos del Noroeste" (fictional Tijuana shop). Say: "This is
what the supplier sees, in their language." Toggle to **中文** to show the Dongguan supplier is
bilingual-first. Change one evidence item from *Not started* to *Uploaded* — watch the readiness
meter move. Say: "They use documents they already have — electricity bills, payroll, a grievance
channel."
Point at the **energy calculator**: "Supplier-specific Scope 3 beats a spend estimate." Compare
the two side-by-side figures.

**1:10 — Standardize only after support lands.**
Point at the **EcoVadis-ready gate** and the support box: funded engineer hours, EcoVadis fee
covered, early-payment finance unlocked at the gate. Say: "We hold everyone to the same EcoVadis
standard — but only once the funded support has landed."

**1:25 — Say only what the data supports (Claims ledger tab).**
Pick "Our supply chain is sustainable" → **Blocked** (FTC Green Guides). Pick "X% of critical
suppliers have reviewed evidence" → **Allowed with qualifier**, with the exact approved wording
auto-filled from live data. Close: "Merit claims publicly only what the evidence tier supports."

## Likely judge questions

**Q: Isn't ignoring your biggest-spend suppliers reckless?**
A: We don't ignore them — they go to "Harvest." Anchor partners (the big US/EU names) already
have EcoVadis scorecards, so we pull that data in parallel; it's nearly free and fast. We spend
the *scarce* resource — funded bilingual engineering support — on the irreplaceable, invisible
suppliers, because those are the ones that can actually stop a product line and that no one else
is looking at. The matrix shows spend and risk are almost orthogonal.

**Q: Why would a 140-worker shop with no sustainability staff ever do this?**
A: Three reasons built into the workspace: it's in their language, it only asks for documents
they already have, and Merit funds it — engineer hours, the EcoVadis fee in year 1, and
sustainability-linked early-payment finance at the gate. The explicit commitments ("data never
used in price negotiations," "no supplier dropped for immature reporting," "payment terms won't
lengthen") remove the fear that participating gets used against them.

**Q: How do you avoid greenwashing while still telling a good story?**
A: The claims ledger enforces it mechanically. Vague claims ("sustainable," "green") are blocked
under the FTC Green Guides; "climate neutral" product claims are blocked under EU Directive
2024/825 (in force from 27 Sept 2026); reduction claims are blocked until two years of
supplier-specific data exist. Coverage claims are allowed *only* with the evidence tier and
period attached — and the exact wording is generated from the live numbers, so the public claim
can never run ahead of the data.
